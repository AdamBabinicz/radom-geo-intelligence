# Privacy Policy - Northstar Ops Geo Hub

Last updated: November 23, 2026
Publisher: Adam Gierczak (100najlepszychfirm@gmail.com)

## Zero-Egress Forge Architecture
Northstar Ops Geo Hub runs exclusively inside Atlassian Forge (Runs on Atlassian, zero external egress).

## Data Access and Storage
- Jira issues, project metadata, and Confluence pages are accessed strictly on behalf of the signed-in user via Forge api.asUser().
- The app does not transmit, store, cache, or sell personal data on any external servers or third-party databases.
- No external tracking pixels, cookies, or telemetry are used.

## Data Deletion and Contact
Uninstalling the app from your Atlassian Cloud site immediately revokes all Forge permissions. For privacy questions, contact 100najlepszychfirm@gmail.com.
